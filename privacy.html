Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 03, 2026. Your privacy and ticket data security are fundamental to our mission.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase event passes, register for a summit, or request production quotes on EventPulse, we collect personal information such as your name, corporate email address, contact phone number, and billing details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We utilize your data strictly to issue encrypted digital tickets, process badge printing, communicate venue entry updates, and provide 24/7 concierge assistance on-site. We never sell your personal information to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All payment transactions are encrypted using AES-256 SSL bank-grade protocols. Digital QR badges feature dynamic anti-duplication tokens to safeguard against ticket scalping.

4. Your Data Rights & CCPA/GDPR Compliance

You maintain full ownership of your data. You have the right to request a complete export of your event order history or request account deletion at any time by contacting our privacy compliance officer at privacy@eventpulse-global.com.
